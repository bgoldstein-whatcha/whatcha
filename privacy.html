Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Whatcha Drinking, Inc. ("Whatcha," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit whatchadrinking.com, follow or message us on social media (including @drinkwhatcha), sign up for updates, enter promotions, or interact with us at events (together, the "Services").

Whatcha Drinking, Inc. is a Delaware corporation and is responsible for your information under this policy. This policy does not cover third-party sites, retailers, or delivery services where you may buy our products; their own privacy policies apply.

Age requirement and children's privacy

Our products contain alcohol, and the Services are intended only for people of legal drinking age (21 or older in the United States). We ask you to confirm your age before entering the site, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21.

If we learn we have collected information from someone under 21, we will delete it. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us at hello@whatchadrinking.com.

We collect only what we need to run the Services, and we do not sell products online or collect payment information on our site.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, we and our service providers automatically collect device and usage information, such as IP address, browser and device type, approximate location derived from your IP address (country, region, city, and postal code), your internet provider or network, pages viewed, referring site, links and buttons clicked, how far you scroll, and time spent. We collect this using cookies, local storage, and similar technologies (see "Cookies and analytics").

Our site analytics assigns your browser a random identifier. If you submit a form on the site, we link the name and email you submit to that identifier, so your activity on the site from that browser may be associated with you.

Information from others

We may receive information from social media platforms (for example, when you tag us), event partners, retailers or distributors (for example, a store contact who asks to carry Whatcha), and analytics providers.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information such as government IDs, health data, or precise geolocation. Please don't send it to us.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Confirm you are of legal drinking age before you enter the site
  • Send updates you signed up for, such as new flavors and where to find us
  • Respond to your questions, partnership requests, retailer inquiries, and job applications
  • Help you find stores that carry Whatcha
  • Run events, sampling, promotions, and ambassador programs
  • Understand how people use the site so we can improve it
  • Market our products to adults 21+, including through social media and advertising
  • Keep the Services secure, prevent fraud and misuse, and fix problems
  • Comply with the law, including alcohol advertising and marketing rules, and enforce our terms

We may also create de-identified or aggregated data (for example, total visits by state) that does not identify you, and use it for any lawful purpose.

Your choices

  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. If you block essential cookies, you may need to confirm your age on each visit.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.
  • You can limit interest-based ads through the Digital Advertising Alliance and the Network Advertising Initiative, and through the ad settings on Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms.

The site does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals, because there is no common standard for them.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only as described here:

  • Service providers who help us run the business, such as website hosting (Vercel), form handling and analytics (Google), database storage (Supabase), and IP address lookup services. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Distribution and retail partners, when you ask about carrying or finding Whatcha (for example, we may pass a retailer inquiry to our distributor in your state).
  • Advertising and social media partners, if we use advertising cookies or pixels. Under some state laws this may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising," which you can opt out of (see "Your privacy rights").
  • Legal and safety reasons, when we believe it is required by law, legal process, or a regulator, or needed to protect the rights, safety, or property of Whatcha, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers, if Whatcha is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets. Your information may be transferred as part of that deal, and this policy will continue to apply to it.
  • With your consent, or at your direction.

Marketing emails and texts

If you sign up for updates, we'll email you about new flavors, where Whatcha is available, events, and promotions. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also opt out by writing to hello@whatchadrinking.com. We may still send non-marketing messages, such as replies to your inquiries.

We will send marketing texts only if you give express consent. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to texts is not required to buy anything, and we do not share your mobile number or texting consent with third parties for their own marketing.

Your privacy rights

Many states, including California, Connecticut, and New Jersey, give residents rights over their personal information. Whether or not a law requires it, we honor the following requests from any U.S. visitor:

  • Access: confirm whether we process your information and get a copy of it
  • Correction: fix information that is inaccurate
  • Deletion: delete information we hold about you
  • Portability: receive your information in a usable format
  • Opt out: of targeted advertising, any "sale" or "sharing" of your information, and profiling with significant effects (we don't do the last one)

How to make a request: email hello@whatchadrinking.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We will verify your request by matching information we already have, such as your email address. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof that the agent has your permission.

We respond within 45 days and will tell you if we need more time, as the law allows. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject "Privacy Appeal." We will respond to appeals within the time the law requires and explain how to contact your state attorney general if you disagree.

We will not discriminate against you, such as by charging different prices or giving you a lower quality of service, for using your privacy rights.

California residents: In the past 12 months, we collected the categories described in "Information we collect" (identifiers, internet activity, approximate location, professional information from job applicants, and inferences for advertising) for the purposes in "How we use information." We do not sell personal information for money; if we use advertising cookies, that may be "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, which you can opt out of by emailing us. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond permitted purposes.

Retention, security, and other details

How long we keep it. We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires. For example: email signups until you unsubscribe; contact and retailer inquiries for up to 2 years; job applications for up to 2 years unless you are hired; analytics data per our providers' settings (generally 14 months or less). Then we delete or de-identify it.

Security. We use reasonable safeguards, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls on our internal dashboard, and limited access to personal information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where your information is stored. The Services are run from the United States and intended for U.S. residents. If you visit from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States.

Links to other sites. The Services link to sites we don't control, such as Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and retailer websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services or the law change. We will post the new version here with a new effective date. If we make material changes, we will give notice on the site or by email before they take effect.
